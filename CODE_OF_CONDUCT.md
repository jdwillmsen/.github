# Code of Conduct

Be decent. Assume good faith, argue about the work rather than the person, and
accept that the maintainer may simply not have time for a thread.

Harassment, personal attacks, and deliberately bad-faith participation are not
welcome and will result in the interaction being locked or blocked without
discussion.

These are personal repositories with one maintainer, so there is no committee
and no appeals process. To raise something, use GitHub's own
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow.
