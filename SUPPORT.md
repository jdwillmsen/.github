# Support

These are personal homelab repositories, published so the work can be read.
They are not a product and they are not maintained for anyone else's use.

**No support is offered.** There is no response-time commitment, no
compatibility promise, and no guarantee that anything here keeps working or
keeps existing. Interfaces change to suit the one operator who runs them.

That said:

- **Questions** — open a discussion or an issue. It may be answered, and it may
  not. Please do not follow up expecting an SLA.
- **Bugs** — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Reports are welcome and
  useful even though fixes are not promised.
- **Security vulnerabilities** — do not open a public issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

If you want to use something here, fork it. The licence
([PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0/))
permits noncommercial use, and a fork you control will outlast whatever these
repositories do next.
